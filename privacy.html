Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

ActionLoop does not collect any personal data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing it records or stores leaves your device unless you send it yourself.

This policy covers ActionLoop for iPhone and ActionLoop for Android from Google Play. Where the two differ, it says so. ActionLoop for Android does not have permission to access the internet at all.

Camera and Microphone

ActionLoop uses your camera and microphone to record video and sound so it can show you a delayed replay. While the app is open, the most recent footage is held temporarily on your device, in memory and in temporary files. It is discarded automatically as recording continues, and anything left over is removed the next time the app starts. It is never uploaded.

On Android, the most recent footage is held in memory only, and is gone when you leave the app. When you save, the clip is written to a temporary file in the app's cache and deleted as soon as it is in your gallery.

Saving Clips

When you tap Save, ActionLoop adds a clip to your Photos library. It asks only for permission to add to your library, so it cannot see the photos and videos already there. On iPhone, from version 1.2, a copy of the clip you saved last is also kept in the app's private storage so you can review it in the app; it is replaced by your next save and removed when the app restarts.

On Android, ActionLoop saves the clip to your gallery, in the Movies/ActionLoop folder. It needs no storage permission and cannot see the photos and videos already there.

Reference Videos

In ActionLoop for iPhone, version 1.2 and later, you can choose a reference video to compare against. The system photo picker gives ActionLoop only the video you choose, and the app keeps a copy in its private storage until you replace or remove it.

Settings

Your settings, such as clip quality and a reference video's mirroring and alignment, are stored on your device.

Problem Reports

From version 2.0, Settings has Report a Problem. It builds a plain text log on your device: the app version and build, the iOS version, the device model, the clip quality, which camera is in use, free storage, the phone's temperature state, whether the microphone is on, whether you have Pro, the last few errors the app noted, and a summary of the crash and hang reports that iOS gave the app. It contains no video, photos, file names of your media or location.

You see the whole log before anything happens, and the app never sends it. Tapping Send opens your own mail app with the log attached (or the share sheet if Mail is not set up), and you decide whether to send it. If you do, it reaches us by email and we use it only to find and fix the problem. If you write to us in another way, we use what you write to reply.

Purchases

From version 2.0, ActionLoop Pro (a monthly or annual subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase) is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account; ActionLoop never sees your payment details or your name. The app only learns from Apple whether you have Pro. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those purchases.

A Guest Pass is an Apple offer code. When you give one, the app opens the system share sheet with a link and you choose where it goes. ActionLoop does not learn who you sent it to or who used it.

Reminders

If you ask for it, ActionLoop sets a notification on your device to remind you that a free trial is about to end. It is scheduled by iOS on the device itself. No server or push service is involved, and nothing about the reminder is sent anywhere.

Update Check

From version 2.0, ActionLoop asks Apple's App Store for the number of its latest version, at most every few hours while you use the app, so it can tell you when an update is available. The request goes to Apple, not to us. It contains nothing about you, your recordings or how you use the app; like any request over the internet, it shows Apple the network address it comes from. Apple's own privacy policy applies.

Diagnostics (Android)

To help fix problems, ActionLoop for Android keeps a log of app events from its last five sessions and reports of its last ten crashes in its private storage. They hold technical details such as your phone model, Android version, app version and camera settings, never your recordings. They leave your device only if you tap Share on the Diagnostics screen, and then only to the app you choose. Clear logs on that screen deletes them.

Deleting Your Data

Deleting ActionLoop removes everything it stores. Clips you saved to Photos or your gallery stay there until you delete them.
