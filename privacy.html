Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026

ActionLoop does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Camera Access

ActionLoop requires access to your device’s camera to provide live video delay functionality. Video is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Data Storage

Any recorded clips are stored locally on your device. ActionLoop does not upload data to external servers.

Analytics

ActionLoop does not use third-party analytics, tracking tools, or advertising frameworks.
